HOW TO PLAY

  • Stand in front of the webcam — each player takes one third of the frame.
  • JUMP: Raise your open hand above your face, or raise your eyebrows quickly.
  • DUCK: Lower your chin toward your chest, or lower your hand below your face.
  • Last player still alive wins!
